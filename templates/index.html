Safe & Transparent Process

The 4-Step Voluntary Donation Journey

Giving blood takes less than 45 minutes from check-in to refreshments, with strict sterile single-use equipment and qualified medical personnel.

1. Book Your Slot

Select a certified hospital and pick a time slot. Prevents waiting queues and controls center capacity.

2. Clinical Screening

Medical staff performs on-site hemoglobin test (Hb ≥ 12.5), weight check (≥ 45kg), and pulse check before donation.

3. Donation (8-10 Mins)

A standardized 350ml or 450ml unit is collected using a sealed, pre-sterilized disposable kit. Completely painless and safe.

4. Saving Up to 3 Lives

Your single unit is processed into Red Blood Cells, Platelets, and Plasma, helping accident victims, surgeries, and cancer patients.
