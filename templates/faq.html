NBTC Standards

Donor Eligibility Guidelines & FAQs

Standards defined by the National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) and Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India.

Mandatory Physical Criteria for Voluntary Donors

Clinical Parameter Required Standard (India) Reason / Medical Rationale
Age 18 to 65 years Legal adulthood and safe physiological tolerance.
Body Weight ≥ 45 kg (for 350ml) • ≥ 55 kg (for 450ml) Ensures blood volume collected remains < 13% of total blood volume.
Hemoglobin (Hb) ≥ 12.5 g/dL Evaluated on-site via finger-prick prior to donation to prevent donor anemia.
Blood Pressure Systolic: 100 - 140 mmHg
Diastolic: 60 - 90 mmHg		 Prevents vasovagal hypotension and cardiovascular stress.
Pulse Rate 60 to 100 beats per minute Regular heart rhythm without arrhythmia.
Donation Interval 90 days (Males) • 120 days (Females) Allows full replenishment of iron reserves and red cell mass.
Temporary Deferral Periods
  • Tattoos / Body Piercings / Acupuncture: Defer for 6 months.
  • Dental Extraction / Minor Surgery: Defer for 3 to 6 months.
  • Antibiotics / Active Fever: Defer until 7 days post-recovery.
  • Alcohol Consumption: Avoid for at least 24 hours prior.
  • Pregnancy & Lactation: Defer for 12 months after delivery.
  • Malaria / Dengue: Defer for 3 months post complete cure.
Permanent Deferrals (Disqualifications)
  • Hepatitis B (HBV) or Hepatitis C (HCV) infection history.
  • HIV 1 & 2 positive test or high-risk exposure.
  • Chronic Cardiac Disease, Stroke, or Hypertension on multiple drugs.
  • Cancer / Malignancy history.
  • Bleeding Disorders (Hemophilia, Thalassemia major).
  • Chronic Kidney or Liver Diseases.

Zero risk. All needles, blood bags, and collection kits are 100% pre-sterilized, disposable, and opened directly in front of you. They are disposed of in biohazard incinerators immediately after use. You cannot contract any disease by giving blood.

The actual blood draw takes only 8 to 10 minutes. The rest of the time (~25 minutes) is spent on donor registration, hemoglobin screening, resting, and enjoying complimentary juice/biscuits in the observation lounge.

In 1996, the Supreme Court of India passed a landmark ruling mandating voluntary blood donation to prevent exploitation of impoverished individuals and ensure the highest safety standards. Studies show voluntary non-remunerated donors have the lowest incidence of transfusion-transmitted infections.
