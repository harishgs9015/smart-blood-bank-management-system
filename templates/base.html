Ethical & Medical Clearance Protocol:

This system is an academic software demonstration for India. Blood group match predictions or automatic scheduling calculations do not constitute medical clearance. All donor health screening, hemoglobin evaluation (Hb ≥ 12.5 g/dL), weight verification (≥ 45kg), cross-matching, and transfusion decisions must be executed on-site by certified transfusion medicine physicians in accordance with the National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) regulations.
